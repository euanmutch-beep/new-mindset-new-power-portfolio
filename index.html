About Euan

With over 35 years of coaching experience and a powerful lived journey of recovery, I specialise in helping people build confidence, break unhelpful habits, and create strong, sustainable routines that genuinely change their lives.

From childhood trauma and years of addiction to becoming a Level 3 Personal Trainer and mental health advocate, my approach blends professional expertise with real-world experience. I’ve supported children, adults, and communities for decades — helping people get fitter, lose weight, improve mental wellbeing, and rebuild belief in themselves.

I am the founder of New Mindset New Power, a movement built on community, accountability, and transformation. We run outdoor bootcamps, women’s peri/post-menopause circuits, men’s mental health groups, and 1-1 personal training — indoors, outdoors, and online.

My mission: Build strong bodies, strong minds, and strong communities — one person at a time.
